Music Adventure / 音樂大冒險 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Music Adventure (音樂大冒險, the App) is a music education hub published by Kenneth. We respect your privacy.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The App does not require an account. Social login is disabled in the shipping build.

On-Device Storage

Player identity (grade / class / name selection), game progress, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device using browser storage APIs. This data never leaves your device unless you back up your device through Apple standard backup services.

Network Access

The App is designed to work offline. It does not load third-party analytics, advertising, or remote fonts.

Audio

The App synthesizes musical tones locally with Web Audio. It does not access your microphone or your Apple Music / media library.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions: kennethchan3868@hotmail.com — also see the Support page.
