Music Adventure / 音樂大冒險 Support

Getting Started

  1. On the hub screen, choose your 年級 / 班別 / 名字 (identity select).
  2. Pick a game card: 音名識辨、節奏識辨、術語識辨 (and other hub games as available).
  3. Use the sound button for the sound panel (music / SFX volume).
  4. Optional: 個人檔案 / 同學名冊 from the hub corner buttons.

No Sound?

  • Ensure the device mute switch is off and volume is up.
  • Open the sound panel and confirm music / 音效 are not muted.
  • Tap once inside the app to unlock audio (iOS Web Audio requires a user gesture).
  • Restart the app if audio still does not play.

Progress and Identity

Scores and identity are saved on this device only. Changing identity or clearing app data resets local progress. There is no cloud account in the App Store build.
